# LinkedIn DM Scripts — Cash Sprint (Sept 6 → Oct 31)

**Goal:** 1 client signed ($5-8k) + 2 recruiter intros to FDE/Applied AI roles within 30 days.

**Cadence:** 5 DMs/day minimum. 100% personalized. Never mass-blast.

**Rules:**
- Always open with a *specific* detail about *them* (recent post, their company, their role).
- Lead with value, not need.
- Single ask per message.
- If no reply in 5 days, one follow-up. Then move on.
- Send during **Tue/Wed/Thu 8-10am CT** (highest response rates).

## SCRIPT 3 — Cold Prospects (Third Priority — volume play)

**Use for:** People NOT in your network but in target ICP. Send as connection request with note (max 300 chars).

> **Hi [First Name] — I'm a production AI builder for GTM (16 systems at Atlas HXM, $9M attributed pipeline). Targeting FDE roles + taking on 1 GTM/AI build this month. Would love to connect.**

If they accept, send Script 1 or Script 2 variant.

## SCRIPT 4 — Follow-Up (5 days after no reply)

> **Hey [First Name] —** Bumping this in case it got buried. If timing's off, totally fine — happy to reconnect in October. Either way, keep killing it at [Company].

If still no reply in 10 days, archive.

## SCRIPT 5 — Post-Signoff / Soft Close (for lukewarm conversations)

Use this when someone says "send me info" or "let's circle back":

> **Perfect — I'll send over the 1-pager today. To make sure it's relevant: what's the biggest manual process in your GTM right now that you'd love to automate? (Even one sentence helps me tailor it.)**

This forces a reply with a real answer — turns passive interest into a real discovery question.
