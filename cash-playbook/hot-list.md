# Hot List — Manual Prospects to Add

Drop contacts here as you discover them. The outreach skill will pull from this when picking tomorrow's batch.

## Format

```
- [Name] @ [Company] — [why they're a fit] — [LinkedIn URL or email]
```

- Jane Smith @ Acme Inc — Founder, raised Series A 6mo ago, scaling GTM — linkedin.com/in/janesmith
- Bob Lee @ TechCorp — Head of Sales, posted about AI pain points — linkedin.com/in/boblee
- recruiter@anthropic.com — Anthropic Talent, looking for FDE — direct email
