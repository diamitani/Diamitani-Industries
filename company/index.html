Principles

How we decide.

The rules that keep a portfolio from becoming a pile.

01

The parent allocates; the businesses execute

Strategy, IP, capital, and shared services sit at the top. Products get one audience, one promise, one owner, one roadmap — and one conversion action.

02

Fund progress, not activity

Every active product has one named owner, one milestone, one budget, one scorecard. Keep, pause, or kill decisions are routine — no more than three active bets at once.

03

Build once, adapt, distribute

Rostr runtime, auth patterns, billing patterns, observability — built once as shared infrastructure. Each product adds its own voice; Salesgency and partnerships distribute it.

04

Separate risk deliberately

An app becomes a company only when separation buys something: outside capital, material risk, repeatable revenue, or a transaction. Until then, product lines with separate books.

05

Consulting funds and proves

Salesgency converts expertise and automation assets into near-term cash, client proof, and reusable product insight. Services fund the portfolio; the portfolio sharpens the services.

06

One promise per brand

The portfolio markets the thesis; product brands market outcomes to their own buyers. Internal modules stay internal until the market demands them.
