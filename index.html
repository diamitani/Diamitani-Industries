Founder

Operator first.

Six years in enterprise sales at Uber and Upwork, then GTM AI & Automation leadership — now building the AI systems revenue teams actually run on.

Patrick Diamitani started selling. Enterprise BDR at Uber for Business ($947K+ gross bookings) and Upwork taught him what revenue teams need — and what they don't. That perspective is why he builds AI differently: every system ships with the operator in mind, not just the architecture.

He is the author of ROSTR, a published framework for production-grade multi-agent systems (Zenodo DOI), and the founder of Salesgency and the Diamitani Industries portfolio.
