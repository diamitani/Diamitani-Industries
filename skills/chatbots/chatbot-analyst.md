# Chatbot Analyst

> Full performance analytics for any chatbot — conversations, MQLs, revenue attribution.

## What It Does
Connects to chatbot data sources and generates performance reports covering conversation volume, qualification rate, meetings booked, deals influenced, and bad/escalated conversation detection. Read-only — never invents numbers.

## Use Cases
- Generate a weekly chatbot performance report
- Measure MQL rate from chat conversations
- Calculate revenue attribution from chatbot leads
- Identify poorly performing conversation branches

## Tech Stack
- HubSpot API
- n8n
- Analytics
- Reporting

## Trigger Phrases
- "how is the chatbot performing"
- "chatbot results"
- "MQLs from chat"
- "show chatbot ROI"
- "chatbot report"

## Setup
Configure `[YOUR_PORTAL_ID]` and `[YOUR_N8N_API_KEY]`. Adjust MQL scoring thresholds for your qualification criteria.
