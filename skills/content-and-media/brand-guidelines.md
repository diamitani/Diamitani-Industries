# Brand Guidelines Enforcer

> Enforces brand voice, visual standards, and consistency across all outputs.

## What It Does
Brand system enforcement skill. Applies brand guidelines to any output — copy, design direction, presentation, or video script. Checks for voice consistency, visual standard compliance, and logo/color/typography rules.

## Use Cases
- Review a piece of copy for brand voice
- Check a deck for visual standard violations
- Enforce tone guidelines in marketing copy
- Brief a vendor on brand requirements

## Tech Stack
- Claude
- Brand System
- Style Guide

## Trigger Phrases
- "check this for brand"
- "brand review"
- "does this follow our guidelines"
- "brand compliance check"

## Setup
Configure `[YOUR_BRAND_VOICE_RULES]`, `[YOUR_COLOR_PALETTE]`, and `[YOUR_TYPOGRAPHY_RULES]` with your own brand guidelines.
