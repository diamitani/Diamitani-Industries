# Presentation Builder

> Builds sales and marketing presentations from structured input.

## What It Does
Sales and marketing presentation builder. Takes deal context, company info, or campaign briefs and generates structured decks for client-facing use. Lighter-weight than presentations-builder — good for quick turnaround decks.

## Use Cases
- Build a sales deck for a specific prospect
- Create a marketing overview for a partner
- Generate a product overview presentation

## Tech Stack
- python-pptx
- Claude
- Presentation Design

## Trigger Phrases
- "build a sales deck"
- "quick presentation for"
- "one-pager slides"
