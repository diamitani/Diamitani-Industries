# AI Prospecting Tester

> Tests and validates AI-generated prospecting outputs against ICP criteria.

## What It Does
QA layer for AI prospecting automation. Validates Clay-generated contacts and outreach against your ICP criteria and messaging standards before they reach reps. Flags non-fits, verifies message quality, and surfaces enrichment gaps.

## Use Cases
- Validate a batch of Clay-generated contacts
- QA outreach copy before it goes live
- Score a prospect list against ICP criteria
- Audit enrichment completeness

## Tech Stack
- Clay
- HubSpot
- Claude
- ICP Logic
