# COF — Cost of Failure

> Quantifies what it costs a prospect to NOT solve the problem.

## What It Does
COF (Cost of Failure) analysis builds a quantified business case by calculating the cost of inaction. Takes a problem statement and maps it to a cost center, quantifies frequency and impact, extrapolates annual cost, and frames the ROI of your solution.

## Use Cases
- Build an executive business case
- Answer 'why should we buy now?'
- Turn soft benefits into hard dollar figures
- Create a ROI calculator for a prospect
