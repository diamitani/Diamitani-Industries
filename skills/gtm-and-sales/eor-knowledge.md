# EOR Knowledge Base

> Employer of Record compliance and country knowledge — answers EOR-specific questions.

## What It Does
Knowledge base skill for Employer of Record (EOR) topics. Answers questions about country-specific compliance requirements, payroll rules, entity readiness, and hiring restrictions. Configure with your own EOR knowledge base documents.

## Use Cases
- Answer country-specific compliance questions
- Check hiring restrictions for a target country
- Brief a sales rep on EOR requirements for a deal
- Validate entity readiness for a new country

## Tech Stack
- Claude
- Knowledge Base
- Compliance Data

## Trigger Phrases
- "what are the EOR requirements for [country]"
- "can we hire in [country]"
- "EOR knowledge for this deal"

## Setup
Populate `[YOUR_EOR_KB_PATH]` with your own EOR compliance documentation. The skill uses RAG to retrieve relevant country context.
