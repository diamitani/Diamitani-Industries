# Factors.ai Analyst

> Pulls intent signals from Factors.ai to surface in-market accounts.

## What It Does
Factors.ai integration skill. Retrieves account-level intent signals and engagement data to prioritize outreach. Ranks accounts by signal strength, maps to CRM records, and formats a prioritized outreach list.

## Use Cases
- Surface in-market accounts from intent data
- Prioritize rep outreach by signal strength
- Map intent signals to CRM records
- Build a daily intent-driven target list

## Tech Stack
- Factors.ai API
- HubSpot
- n8n

## Trigger Phrases
- "show me intent signals"
- "who is showing intent"
- "pull Factors data"
- "intent report for today"

## Setup
Requires `[YOUR_FACTORS_API_KEY]` and `[YOUR_FACTORS_WORKSPACE_ID]`.
