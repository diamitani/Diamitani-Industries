# Proposal Builder

> Builds structured sales proposals and pitch decks from company context.

## What It Does
Autonomous proposal builder. Takes company context, deal notes, and discovery outputs and generates a fully structured sales proposal or pitch deck. Configurable for any company brand and product. Can output to .pptx via python-pptx.

## Use Cases
- Build a proposal for a specific prospect
- Generate a pitch deck from a brief
- Create an internal business case deck
- Draft a partner or investor presentation

## Tech Stack
- Claude
- python-pptx
- ROSTR
- Template Engine

## Trigger Phrases
- "build a proposal"
- "create a deck"
- "make a pitch deck"
- "build me a PowerPoint"
- "proposal for [company]"

## Setup
Configure your company brand template. Set `[YOUR_COMPANY_NAME]`, `[YOUR_TEMPLATE_PATH]`, and `[YOUR_BRAND_COLORS]` in the skill header.
