# Partner PQL Manager

> Classifies partner inquiries, scores PQLs, and routes to the right owner.

## What It Does
AI-powered partner inbound management. Classifies partner form submissions using intent AI, scores product-qualified leads, routes to the correct owner, and silently filters vendor/recruiter spam.

## Use Cases
- Classify and route partner form submissions
- Score partner leads by PQL criteria
- Auto-draft responses to qualified partner inquiries
- Filter spam from the partner inbound queue

## Tech Stack
- HubSpot
- n8n
- Claude
- Lead Scoring Logic

## Trigger Phrases
- "manage partner leads"
- "score these PQLs"
- "route partner inquiries"
- "classify this partner submission"

## Setup
Configure your PQL scoring criteria and routing rules. Replace `[PARTNER_TEAM_OWNER]` with the correct HubSpot user ID.
