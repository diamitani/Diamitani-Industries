# RFP Knowledge Base Builder

> Parses RFP documents into atomic Q/A pairs — the foundation of your answer library.

## What It Does
Phase 1 of the RFP Suite. Uploads any RFP document and parses every question into atomic Q/A entries. Classifies by topic, assigns owners, flags conflicts with existing answers, and exports to an Excel workbook for review.

## Use Cases
- Parse a new RFP into an answer library
- Identify gaps in your existing RFP knowledge base
- Assign question ownership across subject matter experts
- Export a structured workbook for team review
